About sound and alarms

A website cannot force an alarm sound when it is fully closed. Android and Chrome decide notification sound, vibration and importance. The app requests an alarm-style notification with vibration. While this page is open, it also plays a short alert tone.

Allow notifications when Chrome asks. If no sound is heard, check Android Settings → Apps → Chrome → Notifications → this site, and turn off silent mode.

Notifications are sent only on the correct trip direction and only once per trip. The estimate does not include live traffic.
